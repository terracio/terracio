# Tarig Bouazzati — Enterprise AI Adoption & Deployment

Most enterprise AI work does not fail at the demo. It fails in the gap between a promising prototype and a workflow that people actually trust, adopt, and use every day.

That gap is where I *love* to work.

I help teams find the right operational problems, map them into AI-enabled systems, build the first working versions, align the stakeholders around them, and roll them out with enough evaluation, governance, and business clarity to make adoption real.

I have done this as a technical founder, product owner, enterprise deployment lead, and teacher — across contact centers, customer support, operations, and workflow automation.

---

## What I Do

I work at the intersection of:

- **Workflow discovery** — finding the real operational bottleneck behind the requested AI solution.
- **Technical deployment** — building production-shaped systems with clear interfaces, tools, policies, and rollout paths.
- **Enterprise adoption** — aligning operators, engineers, product teams, security, and executives around measurable value.
- **Evaluation and trust** — using scorecards, evidence, human-in-the-loop controls, and phased rollout to reduce risk.
- **Enablement** — helping teams understand what the technology can do, where it breaks, and how to use it well.

I enjoy staying close to the work: talking to users, mapping systems, writing code, testing failure modes, and turning abstract AI capability into something useful inside real organizations.

## Featured Project

### [MealPlan VoiceOps](https://github.com/terracio/voice-ops-agent)

A production-shaped realtime voice operations system for a fictional meal-plan company.

It explores how a voice AI system can safely handle contact-center workflows where mistakes affect deliveries, payments, dietary constraints, and customer trust.

> The model can propose. The application decides.

This project reflects how I think enterprise AI systems should be built: the model handles language and reasoning, while the application owns authority, state, policy, and accountability.
